Matthew Prock

Hi, I'm Matt! I currently work in software development in Detroit, but recently graduated with my Bachelor of Science from the University of Michigan; I double majored in Computer Science and Cognitive Science, and minored in Art & Design. I love programming and find our brains fascinating, which informed my academic interest in the intersections between tech and cognition, human-computer interaction, and accessibility.
In my spare time, you'll most likely find me reading, drawing, spending time with family and friends, or taking a long and meandering walks.
